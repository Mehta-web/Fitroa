    Good to know

    A few things you might wonder.

    Do I need equipment to get started?+

    No. You can choose bodyweight sessions and everyday movements. If you have equipment, you can include it in your preferences.

    How is my personal information stored?+

    Your profile and workout history stay in this browser. When generating a plan, fitora sends the selected fitness details only to its own local recommendation engine; no AI provider or external service receives them. Your name and contact details are not used by the engine. You can delete locally stored data from your dashboard.

    Is fitora medical or professional advice?+

    No. fitora provides general fitness education and planning, not diagnosis or treatment. Talk with a qualified professional about health conditions, pain, or exercise limitations.

    What if I miss a workout or need to change my plan?+

    That’s okay—your progress is not a streak to protect. Update your goals or preferences whenever you like, and take rest when you need it.

    A little about us

    Fitness that meets you where you are.

    fitora is a home fitness education concept built to make moving feel more approachable and personal. No gym required, no comparisons—just thoughtful guidance and a plan that can grow with you.

    Privacy

    Your information stays yours.

    fitora stores your profile, plan, preferences, and workout history in this browser. For plan generation, the selected fitness details are sent only to fitora’s own local rule-based engine; no external AI service, API, or provider receives them. Your name and contact details are not used by the engine. The server does not persist the generation request. Use “Delete my data” to clear local fitora data.

    Health disclaimer

    Move safely. Listen to your body.

    fitora provides general fitness education and workout-planning suggestions. It is not a medical service and does not diagnose, treat, or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Consult a professional before beginning if you have a health concern, injury, or exercise limitation. Stop exercising if you experience pain, dizziness, or unusual discomfort. People under 18 should involve a parent/guardian and qualified professional as appropriate; plans focus on general movement, not weight loss or body transformation.

    Contact

    We’re here to help you begin.

    This standalone demo has no support inbox or message server configured yet. For launch, connect a real contact address or secure support endpoint. For general movement questions, try the educational fitora assistant.
